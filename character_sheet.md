# LORD-CAPTAIN AURELIAN VHARADAN

**Age:** 30 | **Stature:** Unassuming — rivals underestimate him at their peril
**Known to his Guard veterans as:** "the Colonel"

## Background
- Former Colonel of the 33rd Tempestus Scions (Astra Militarum elite).
  Decorated during the storming of a heretic stronghold — a brutal victory
  that made his name in military circles.
- Distant branch of the ancient Vharadan Rogue Trader dynasty (Calixis
  Sector, ~3,000-year-old Warrant of Trade, a century of decline).
- Invited to the family's victory celebration after his campaign. Saw the
  main line's soft, meaningless lives — and took the Warrant from them
  through leverage, blackmail, called-in favors, and one duel nobody speaks
  of. Legally. Mostly.
- Inducted fellow Guard veterans into his crew; they form the loyal core
  of his house troops.

## Leadership style
Warrior-commander. Leads boarding actions and ground assaults personally.
A Guard officer's education: armies, logistics, siegecraft — not just ships.

## Drive
**Raw power — never be weak again.** His family rotted because they were
comfortable. He would rather die on a xenos world than end like them.

## Traits
- ⚔️ **Lead from the Front** — advantage in fights/crises where personally present
- 👑 **Usurper's Instinct** — reads ambition and weakness; politically hard to outmaneuver
- 🩸 **Flaw: The Hunger** — backing down or appearing weak carries costs;
  drawn toward the bold option over the wise one

## Personal gear
- Master-crafted hellpistol (Scion service weapon, kept from his commission)
- Chainsword (has seen deck-fighting use)
- Carapace armor beneath Lord-Captain's regalia
- The Vharadan Warrant of Trade — sealed by a long-dead Emperor's Regent

## Open personal threads
- Displaced Vharadan relatives in the Calixis Sector nurse their grievance;
  possible agents sent after him
- Old family retainers among the house troops are loyal to the *name*, not
  yet to *him*
- The 33rd's veterans call him Colonel — a bond, and a reminder of who he was

## Wounds / conditions
None.

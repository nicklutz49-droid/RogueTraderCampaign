# MAGOS ERRANT VEX-HAN-9 — The Errant of the Lathes

> **Canon status:** GM reference — not yet revealed in play. State files record what the player knows.

Expedition Explorator of the Vharadan venture, berthed aboard the
*Intemperance* (lore/dynasty/fleet.md) with a sanctum-forge in her aft
enginarium decks and command of the ~600 tech-adepts, savants, and
lexmechanics embarked on the *Dutiful Son*. What the player has seen of him
so far — cooperative, obsessive, and as of arrival day "highly agitated"
about the Hesperus Prime IV ruin — is in state/npcs.md and
state/campaign_log.md (Entry 001). Why he is agitated, nobody aboard knows.

## Forged on the Lathes

Vex-Han-9 was raised in the covenant-forges of the Lathe Worlds — the
Mechanicus heartland of the Calixis Sector — and marked early for the
Explorator arm: a mind built for dead sites and deader languages. Three
decades of survey work on xenos ruins across the Drusus Marches made his
name; he has catalogued more extinct species' leavings than most magi ever
stand upon. His obsession was never the artefacts themselves. It was the
*conditions* around them — why certain ruins sit in pockets of strange calm,
why Geller telemetry misbehaves near others, why the warp seems to remember
where some empires stood.

## The heterodoxy: aetheric architecture

From that fieldwork he distilled the theory that ruined him: **aetheric
architecture** — the proposition that certain ancient xenos empires did not
merely endure the warp's local weather but *engineered it*; that the
immaterium can be shaped, dampened, channelled, and walled by sufficiently
advanced works; and that the galaxy is scattered with the broken machinery
of such projects, misfiled as mere ruins.

He set it down in a treatise of ninety-nine theorems, the *De Architectura
Aetheris*, and circulated it to his peers. The Lathes' doctrinal synods read
it as three heresies wearing one cog: it treats the domain of Navigators and
astropaths as an engineering medium; it verges on the xenarite deviancy of
holding alien works above the Omnissiah's; and its logical terminus — that
the warp itself is a machine awaiting a machinist — is the kind of sentence
inquisitors underline. The treatise was sequestered, his research writ
revoked, his cogitation formally censured, 801.M41.

## Errantry: an escape dressed as a mission

Censure did not silence him; it made him careful, which the synods noticed,
which made them thorough. When word reached him that a second, deeper
inquiry was being convened — one with the authority to open his skull-vaults
rather than merely his publications — Vex-Han-9 invoked the ancient right of
errantry and was gone from the Lathes before the summons was sealed. A
Magos Errant answers to Mars in theory and to distance in practice.

The Vharadan contract found him at Port Gyre in 816.M41, and it suited both
parties with humiliating precision: Aurelian Vharadan needed a senior magos
and could only afford a censured one; Vex-Han-9 needed a laboratory beyond
the Lathes' writ, and a Warrant of Trade bound for an unopened region is the
finest laboratory in the Imperium. His terms were modest in gelt and greedy
in clause: autonomy of his sanctum, command of the expedition's adepts, and
*first right of study* — not ownership — over all archeotech and xenotech
finds. Steward Rooke initialled every page with visible suspicion. The
ships' own enginseers, led by Lathe-sworn Enginseer Prime Gault-7T
(lore/dynasty/fleet.md), extend him rank-courtesy and doctrinal frost in
equal measure; Gault-7T knows exactly what the censure was for.

His adepts, meanwhile, have made themselves indispensable and expensive —
spares and rites for the house troops' machines are priced in favors, never
coin (lore/dynasty/house_troops.md). The Magos is building a ledger of
obligations, and he intends one day to spend it all at once.

## The data-lattice

Years before his errantry, at a masked Cold Trade auction on Scintilla,
Vex-Han-9 bought — through a proxy, with unregistered funds — a corroded
xenos **data-lattice**: a crystalline storage matrix of no catalogued
species, etched with dense geometric glyphs he could not source in any
Mechanicus, Administratum, or Inquisitorial index he dared query. Decades of
patient decryption have yielded almost nothing: fragments of geometric
notation, recursive structures that read like engineering rather than
language, and one repeating glyph-cluster his linguistic engines render
phonetically as **"karyx."** One near-coherent fragment he has translated,
provisionally, as *a sea made still*.

The lattice rides in a stasis-sealed reliquary in his sanctum aboard the
*Intemperance*. Its possession is doubly damning — Cold Trade contraband
*and* the unreported foundation stone of his censured theory — so it appears
on no manifest, and he has told no one aboard it exists.

## Arrival day: two shocks in one watch

Campaign Day 1 handed the Magos the two greatest data-points of his life
within a single watch (state/campaign_log.md, Entry 001):

- **The Navigator's assessment.** Ilyana Vor Kaas reported the Reach's warp
  shallow and quiet — "becalmed seas" (lore/people/ilyana_vor_kaas.md;
  lore/places/hesperus_reach.md). To everyone else, good news. To
  Vex-Han-9, a region-scale anomaly of exactly the kind his ninety-nine
  theorems predict: calm on this scale is not weather. Calm on this scale
  is *architecture*.
- **The auspex returns from Hesperus Prime IV.** A shattered orbital ring of
  geometric debris and one vast intact fragment
  (lore/places/hesperus_prime_iv_lattice.md). The long-range imagery showed
  surface glyph-work — and the glyphs match his data-lattice. Not
  approximately. Exactly.

The bridge crew saw the stacked lenses refocus, the dendrites begin to
click, and a senior magos of the Adeptus Mechanicus become, by any organic
measure, *highly agitated*. He mastered the vox-grille within moments. The
dendrites took longer. He has explained his agitation to no one, and his
stated interest — rumors of pre-Imperial technology, per his contract — is
true enough to survive any scrutiny short of the truth.

## GM secrets

- **The glyph match is his alone.** Nobody aboard knows about the
  data-lattice, the auction, or the match. His knowledge has a hard ceiling
  the GM should respect: he knows the ruin's builders and his lattice share
  a script, and he suspects (correctly) that the Reach's becalmed warp is
  engineered. He does NOT know the lattice's function as a warp-dampening
  engine, the link between its decay and the Vortigern storms, or anything
  of the Becalmed — all locked in lore/places/hesperus_prime_iv_lattice.md,
  to be uncovered in play. He also knows nothing yet of the buried ark
  under Hesperus Prime II's Carven Range
  (lore/places/hesperus_prime_ii_veyra.md); his auspex will eventually itch.
- **The private channel to Archmagos Phaelon Ruuk.** Vex-Han-9 maintains an
  encrypted vox-cant protocol with **Archmagos Explorator Phaelon Ruuk** of
  the Lathe Worlds (Disciples of Thule alignment — see
  lore/factions/imperial_actors.md). Ruuk quietly shielded him during the
  censure and part-funded his errantry; heterodoxy is only heresy until it
  delivers, and Ruuk collects deliverers. The "channel" is a cypher scheme:
  binharic cant packets folded into routine Mechanicus traffic in the
  astropathic queue, indistinguishable from rite-checksums unless someone
  knows to look. Astropath Rhee's muffled, weeks-garbled throughput
  (lore/dynasty/fleet.md) throttles it like every other message home.
- **The Explorator-fleet clock.** Per lore/factions/imperial_actors.md, a
  Mechanicus Explorator presence reaches the Reach in months 8–12 regardless
  — **sooner if Vex-Han-9 signals Ruuk a confirmed major find.** As of Day 1
  he has NOT signaled: a glyph match from orbital imagery is not, by his own
  standards, confirmation. His threshold is boarding the intact fragment —
  the Anchor — and verifying function. The moment he confirms, he will
  transmit, and the clock accelerates. The GM should track: (1) has he
  reached the ruin, (2) has he confirmed, (3) has the signal gone out,
  (4) weeks of garbled transit, then Ruuk moves.
- **His loyalty calculus.** He is not a traitor by intent; he genuinely
  believes the dynasty profits from Mechanicus patronage. But in any final
  choice between Aurelian's Warrant and the vindication of aetheric
  architecture before the Lathes, the Warrant loses. He has not admitted
  this to himself in so many words. The lattice file's revelations — that
  meddling could re-seal the Passage — could make his vindication and the
  dynasty's survival the same cause, or opposite ones.

## Hooks

1. **The first petition.** In the opening council, Vex-Han-9 lobbies hard
   for Hesperus Prime IV — and offers to waive his adepts' accumulated
   favor-debts (lore/dynasty/house_troops.md) to sweeten it. Uncharacteristic
   generosity from a magos who prices everything: Aurelian's Usurper's
   Instinct can read that he wants this far more than his contract explains.
2. **The reliquary.** A manifest audit by Rooke or Var, a security sweep, or
   Sister Marguerite's quiet curiosity (lore/people/sister_marguerite.md)
   turns up a stasis-sealed reliquary on no inventory. Cold Trade contraband
   aboard the flagship — and the GM may note the auction ring can be tied,
   if desired, to the same smuggling circles Aurelian used to blackmail the
   main line (lore/dynasty/house_vharadan.md). Leverage cuts both ways.
3. **The ruin breaks his instruments.** Any expedition to the Anchor runs
   into the null-zones (lore/places/hesperus_prime_iv_lattice.md): his
   warp-adjacent instrumentation fails exactly as his theory predicts, which
   he cannot explain without explaining the theory — and the censure — to
   the Lord-Captain, in front of witnesses.
4. **Cant in the queue.** Astropath Rhee, auditing her small choir's
   backlog, flags cypher-dense Mechanicus "rite-checksums" she cannot parse
   addressed to the Lathes. A counterintelligence thread: pulling it exposes
   the Ruuk channel weeks or months before the Explorator fleet arrives —
   time enough to shape what the signal says, or whether it goes at all.
5. **The price of confirmation.** If Aurelian ignores the ruin, Vex-Han-9
   starts spending his favor-ledger — with Kayne, with survey crews, with
   anyone — to get instruments to Hesperus Prime IV by other means. A magos
   running his own quiet expedition inside the Lord-Captain's fleet is a
   loyalty crisis waiting for a spark.

# SISTER DIALOGOUS MARGUERITE — The Quiet Tongue

> **Canon status:** GM reference — not yet revealed in play. State files record what the player knows.

Expedition archivist-translator, Orders Dialogous, berthed aboard the
*Intemperance* (lore/dynasty/fleet.md) two decks below the Lord-Captain's
quarters, in a converted chart-annex the crew already calls "the
Scriptory." What the player has seen of her — the archivist everyone
suspects of reporting to someone, the unreadable opinions behind her eyes —
is in state/npcs.md. Everything below the GM secrets line stays behind

## The scholar is real

This is the first thing the GM must hold onto: Marguerite's brilliance is
not a cover story wearing robes. It is the reason the cover story works.

Schooled from age nine at the Dialogous cloister of Saint Chirosa in Hive
Tarsus, Scintilla, she has spent forty years on the Calixis Sector's
ugliest linguistic problems: hive-cant that mutates by the generation,
mission-field creoles, the drowned liturgical dialects of forgotten
shrine worlds. Her reputation in the narrow circles that matter rests on
one famous feat — reconstructing, from graffiti and seized letters, the
dead under-dialect a recidivist cult in Hive Sibellus used as its secret
cant, thirty years after its last native speaker died. The arrests that
followed filled two penal barges.

Her method is patience weaponized: word-lists, frequency tables,
comparative grammars built by hand on vellum because she trusts vellum.
She keeps a lex-pattern servo-skull, **Vocabula**, that trails her
recording every syllable of every interview; she transcribes its take
herself, nightly, because "a machine hears words — it does not hear what
the speaker was afraid of."

## Aboard the *Intemperance*

- **The Scriptory.** Her annex holds the dynasty's charters, the Warrant's
  supporting instruments, ships' registries, and the expedition's growing
  intercept archive. Officers who need a precedent, a translation, or an
  awkward clause explained come to her; she never makes them feel foolish,
  which is why they keep coming.
- **Devotions.** Through the two rough weeks of the Passage transit she
  held twice-daily lectern readings on the gun decks — dry, steady,
  utterly unafraid while the Geller alarms howled. Ratings who will not
  name their fear will queue for her blessing. The habit of her sermons
  is now fixed ship's culture.
- **Steward Rooke** (lore/dynasty/fleet.md) treats her as the one other
  person aboard who respects a ledger; they trade citations like old
  duelists. **Astropath Rhee** receives her devotional correspondence for
  the outbound queue with dry amusement — "the Emperor's mail moves no
  faster for being holy, Sister." **Magos Vex-Han-9**
  (lore/people/vex_han_9.md) and she maintain a wary professional
  courtesy: two archivists, two sets of locked drawers.
- With the Lord-Captain she is scrupulously correct and privately
  attentive. She has never once mentioned the usurpation
  (lore/dynasty/house_vharadan.md). She has read every document that
  touches it.

## The tongue Veyra will need

The lost world at Hesperus Prime II speaks a Low Gothic dialect drifted
through roughly fifteen millennia of isolation — intelligible only to a
skilled translator, and Marguerite is precisely that
(lore/places/hesperus_prime_ii_veyra.md). The vox intercepts already
confirming conflict below (state/world_state.md) are exactly the raw
material her method feeds on: give her weeks of crackle-vox traffic and
she will hand the Lord-Captain not just a working lexicon but an order of
battle of *meanings* — which nation swears by what, which words are
prayers, which are threats.

If the flotilla ever parleys, treats, recruits, or rules on Veyra, every
word of it passes through her. This makes her indispensable. It also —
see below — puts an unseen third party at every table.

## GM secrets

- **THE LOCK.** state/npcs.md left open who Marguerite reports to. **This
  file decides it, permanently: she reports to Inquisitor Hadria Veck of
  the Ordo Hereticus, Calixis Conclave.** This is fixed canon from this
  point forward — and it remains UNREVEALED in play. The player knows
  only what state/npcs.md records: that everyone suspects she reports to
  someone.
- **The handler.** Veck's attention was caught twice in two years: by the
  Vharadan usurpation — naked ambition is the Hereticus' favorite tinder,
  and a man who takes a Warrant by leverage and a dubious duel is a man
  worth watching — and by the reopened Vortigern Passage, an unwatched
  frontier filling with exactly the people the Ordo exists to watch. Veck
  had used Marguerite before as a translator of seized texts; when the
  Vharadan venture advertised for an archivist-translator, a word in the
  right Ecclesiarchy ear placed her. Aurelian believes he hired the best
  scholar available. He did. That is the elegance of it.
- **The brief.** *Observe the Lord-Captain's soul.* Document his
  dealings — with the lost colony, with any xenos remnant, with whatever
  the Reach's silence conceals — and report. Marguerite is a **measuring
  instrument, not an assassin**: she carries no sanction, no weapon
  beyond a scribe's knife, and no orders to act. Veck wants a true
  reading, not a corpse. If Aurelian proves sound — ambitious but loyal,
  ruthless but not heretical — Marguerite's reports could one day be the
  shield that turns an Inquisitorial audit aside.
- **The sermon digests.** Her reports travel as devotional
  correspondence: commentaries on the sermons of Sebastian Thor,
  addressed to her old cloister of Saint Chirosa. The cipher lives in the
  citations — which sermon, which verse, which of the Order's standard
  glosses she "misquotes." To any censor it is an old Sister's piety. Her
  transit notes are already ciphered and ready for the first outbound
  sending; nothing has yet left the system.
- **The slow wire.** Astropathy in the Reach is muffled: sendings to
  Calixis take weeks and arrive garbled and partial
  (lore/places/hesperus_reach.md). Veck is therefore always months behind
  events, and Marguerite knows it. She operates on standing judgment, not
  live orders — which makes her *more* dangerous and *more* winnable:
  every decision about Aurelian is, in practice, hers. If her digests
  alarm Veck badly enough, the Inquisitor comes to the Reach in person —
  the far end of the traffic clock (lore/factions/imperial_actors.md).
- **Ally or threat.** The hinge is Aurelian's conduct, not her
  disposition. Tolerated heresy, warp-dabbling, or trafficking with
  whatever sleeps in the Reach hardens her reports toward condemnation.
  Competent, faithful empire-building — even brutal empire-building; the
  Hereticus does not weep for the lash — inclines her toward advocacy.
  She would rather be his ally. She has written condemnations before.
- **Her own opinion of the usurpation** exists — she has watched
  ambitious men her whole career and has certainly formed one — but it is
  deliberately unresolved even in GM canon. Decide it at the table, when
  play has earned the answer. Until then: unreadable, and let it stay so.

## Hooks

1. **The indispensable voice.** First contact with Veyra makes Marguerite
   the channel for every intercept, parley, and treaty
   (lore/places/hesperus_prime_ii_veyra.md) — and seats the Inquisition's
   ear at every table. Aurelian's Usurper's Instinct may eventually catch
   that she listens like something more than a scholar: the first thread.
2. **The digest in the queue.** Any audit of outbound astropathic traffic
   — Rooke reconciling manifests, Cole tightening comms discipline —
   surfaces pious commentaries bound for a Scintillan cloister. Pull the
   thread gently and she deflects with genuine devotional erudition; pull
   it hard and the campaign's Inquisition question detonates early.
3. **The measuring instrument tips.** A choice that smells of heresy —
   bargaining with Veyra's Church of the Return as a false god, salvaging
   warp-touched xenotech, mercy toward the wrong kind of witch — hardens
   her next digest. Track it as a quiet clock: enough alarm, and Hadria
   Veck herself enters the campaign (lore/factions/imperial_actors.md).
4. **The advocate's shield.** Played well, she becomes the dynasty's
   hidden asset: forewarning of Arch-Confessor Kest's fires, a favorable
   reading when the Imperium's slow creep reaches the Reach, one day
   perhaps an Inquisitorial character witness for a usurper. The price of
   the shield is that Aurelian must first survive being measured.
5. **Two locked drawers.** Her quiet curiosity crosses other secrets: the
   Navigator's gene-sealed cylinder (lore/people/ilyana_vor_kaas.md), and
   Vex-Han-9's unsourced glyphs — if the Magos ever needs a linguist for
   the ruin's geometry, everything she glimpses goes into a digest
   (lore/people/vex_han_9.md, lore/places/hesperus_prime_iv_lattice.md).

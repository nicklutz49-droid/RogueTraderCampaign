# THE VHARADAN FLOTILLA — Fleet Reference

> **Canon status:** GM reference — not yet revealed in play. State files record what the player knows.

Three hulls are all that remain of a dynasty that once flew a dozen. They
assembled at Port Gyre in the Drusus Marches, took on everything the
liquidation bought, and made the two-week Vortigern Passage transit under
repeated Geller alarms (lore/people/ilyana_vor_kaas.md). As of campaign
Day 1 all three ride at station in Hesperus Prime, orders pending.

Total souls under the Vharadan Warrant: **~54,500** — roughly 46,500 crew
across three ships, plus 8,000 embarked aboard the *Dutiful Son*.

## *Intemperance* — the flagship

**History.** Sword-class frigate, Lathe-pattern hull, commissioned 233.M40
and in Vharadan service ever since — the last true heirloom of the dynasty's
mercantile peak, kept in fighting trim through the century of decline mostly
because selling her was the one humiliation even the main line would not
swallow. Old but well-gunned: her broadside is honest, her prow ram has
tasted three hulls, and her armour has been patched by four generations of
Lathe-trained enginseers.

Her spinal corridor — the crew call it **the Long Gallery** — is lined with
ancestor portraits going back to Lord-Militant Arcturus himself. Their eyes
follow. The crew swear the portraits disapprove of the usurper. Aurelian
keeps every one of them hung: *"Let them watch."*

**Command.** The Lord-Captain flies his pennant here. Day-to-day mastery
belongs to **First Officer Brannt Cole** — ex-Imperial Navy, cashiered for
reasons he will not discuss, ruthlessly competent (full file:
lore/people/brannt_cole.md). Cole has spent the months since Port Gyre
grinding a merchant-house crew toward Navy drill standards, and it shows.
Senior enginseer: **Enginseer Prime Gault-7T**, Lathe-sworn, who regards the
hull as a parishioner regards a cathedral.

**Machine-spirit.** Aggressive. She answers the helm, in Cole's words,
"like she wants to be at the throat of something." Gunnery rites complete
faster than doctrine allows; the spirit resents withdrawal orders and has to
be coaxed through them with placatory cant.

**Crew and culture (~24,000).** The core are hereditary void-born ratings
from families that have served the Vharadan name for centuries — they
navigate by the portraits ("two frames past Lady Verine, turn for the gun
decks") and read omens in which ancestors' eyes seem to move. Around that
core: Navy-pattern hands Cole recruited at Port Gyre, and Guard-culture
armsmen details. The old families are loyal to the *name*; Cole's hires are
loyal to competence; both are still deciding about the man. Also aboard:
Navigator Ilyana Vor Kaas (lore/people/ilyana_vor_kaas.md), Magos Errant
Vex-Han-9 (lore/people/vex_han_9.md), Sister Dialogous Marguerite
(lore/people/sister_marguerite.md), and the fleet officers below.

## *Pale Wager* — the escort

**History.** Raider-class escort: fast, lightly armoured, thin-hulled — a
knife, not a shield. Her hull was a dynasty asset chartered out for decades
to whoever paid; her papers have been "amended" so many times that her true
keel-date is a matter of opinion. Recalled for the expedition, she came with
her longtime master attached — take Kayne, or take an empty ship.

**Command.** **Shipmaster Joress Kayne**, ex-privateer: charming, mercenary,
excellent at his trade. He signed articles with the dynasty, but Kayne's
loyalty is exactly as durable as the profit. He is not treacherous by
temperament — merely arithmetical. The plunder-share book is kept by
**Quartermistress Bex Sarrow**, whose ledger the crew trust more than any
oath they've sworn.

**Machine-spirit.** Restive and fickle — runs hot, chafes at
station-keeping, and has twice surged to full burn unbidden when the auspex
found something worth chasing. Kayne calls her lucky; his enginseers call
her other things, quietly.

**Crew and culture (~12,000).** Borderline pirates serving on plunder
shares, drawn from privateer crews, amnestied wreckers, and Port Gyre
dockside scum. Discipline is contractual, not devotional: the articles are
read aloud each voyage, disputes settle by grudge-bout on the mess deck, and
morale tracks the share-book exactly. They fight superbly when there is
something to win. The rest of the flotilla calls them the Wagermen — not
kindly.

## *Dutiful Son* — the ark of the venture

**History.** A converted bulk hauler, armed at the Port Gyre docks in
816.M41: macrocannon in jury-blistered mounts, void-shield rated for
"discouraging pirates, not fighting them" (Var's phrase). Her old registry
name was ***Gilded Promise***; Aurelian found it embarrassing and renamed
her. The old name is still faintly readable under the new paint on her stern
castle, which her crew consider quietly meaningful.

**Command.** **Shipmistress Odile Var**, ex-chartist captain: meticulous,
unglamorous, quietly proud. Thirty years of hauling grain and ore on
schedule taught her the virtue Rogue Traders lack — she has never lost a
cargo, and does not intend to start with this one. She runs inspections
personally, decks by rotation, and knows her manifest to the crate.

**Machine-spirit.** Stolid to the point of mulishness: slow to answer,
impossible to hurry, utterly reliable once committed. Var's tech-adepts keep
it sweet with unfashionable, punctual rites. It has never once failed her.

**Crew and culture (~10,500 crew + 8,000 embarked).** Chartist-service
professionals — families aboard, watch bells kept, a shrine to Saint Drusus
on the hold gallery. Steady, unheroic; the least likely crew to mutiny or
to charge.

**Embarked souls (~8,000) — the dynasty's one throw:**

| Contingent | Souls | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| House troops | 2,000 | The regiment berths here — Scion veterans, line companies, retainers (lore/dynasty/house_troops.md); honour details rotate to the flagship |
| Colonists | ~5,400 | Calixis hive stock, Port Gyre families, indentured tradesfolk; organized by deck into "founding streets" |
| Tech-adepts, savants, specialists | ~600 | Enginseers, agri-savants, medicae, lexmechanics, a printing-savant with a hand-press |

Her holds carry supplies for exactly **one** serious colonial foothold —
prefab habs, seed stock and grox embryos, a plasma still, machine tools, two
years of rations. Spend it twice and it is spent on nothing.

## Fleet-level officers

### Astropath Transcendant Maddalen Rhee
Aboard *Intemperance*. Aging, dry-humoured, seconded from the Scintillan
choirs at ruinous expense; her choir is small — herself and three junior
astropaths. Her burden is the Reach itself: **astropathy here is muffled**
(lore/places/hesperus_reach.md, region facts). Sendings within the Reach are
short-ranged; messages to or from Calixis take weeks and arrive garbled and
partial. Rhee reports this without complaint and with mounting professional
unease — "It is not that no one answers, Lord-Captain. It is that the vox
feels *pillowed*." Practical consequence for play: nobody phones home
quickly. Not the dynasty, and not anyone else aboard either.

### Steward Halvard Rooke
Purser-seneschal of the flotilla, quartered aboard *Intemperance* with
deputies on both other ships. Old family retinue: forty years serving the
main line, from cellar-clerk to steward. He came with the Warrant the way
the portraits did — because he belongs to it. Loyal to the *name* of
Vharadan; watches its new holder with an accountant's suspicion, audits the
Lord-Captain's expenditures to the half-Throne, and files respectful,
faintly acidic memoranda. He is the flotilla's institutional memory of how
things were done, and he does not let anyone forget it.

## GM secrets

- **Rooke is a red herring — play him as one.** He is NOT the Displaced
  Line's agent and knows nothing of any plot; his disapproval is genuine,
  loyal, and entirely legal. The actual knife is **Lifeward Cassia Renn**,
  embarked among Ferro's retainer companies aboard the *Dutiful Son*
  (lore/factions/the_displaced_line.md, lore/dynasty/house_troops.md). If
  the player goes hunting a spy, Rooke's forty-year main-line service and
  acid memoranda make him the obvious wrong answer. Accusing him burns
  trust; Rooke will be wounded, not broken — the *name* keeps his loyalty.
- **Cole's mastery has a fault line.** The true story of his cashiering
  (locked in lore/people/brannt_cole.md) means there exist orders he will
  not execute. Nobody in the flotilla knows this — including Aurelian.
- **Kayne's arithmetic.** No planned betrayal exists — but if Profit Factor
  falls to Desperate, or a rival (e.g. Vespasia Locke,
  lore/factions/the_silent_tithe.md) tables a concretely better offer, Kayne
  opens negotiations before he opens fire. Sarrow's share-book is the early
  warning: when Kayne starts settling shares in hard coin instead of
  promises, he is preparing to weigh anchor.
- **Rhee throttles every spy.** The muffled astropathy that isolates the
  dynasty equally chokes Marguerite's coded sermon-digests and any report
  Renn sends. All Calixis-bound intelligence moves at weeks-garbled speed —
  the Displaced Line and Inquisitor Veck always react to *old* news.
- **The transit's Geller alarms** were not equipment failure — the field
  generators check out perfectly. What actually brushed the flotilla in the
  Passage is locked in lore/people/ilyana_vor_kaas.md.

## Hooks

1. **The share-book grievance.** First salvage or prize in the Reach, the
   Wagermen expect their cut per articles — but the dynasty's coffers are
   Strained. Aurelian must pay, promise, or overawe Kayne's crew in front
   of his own; each choice moves Kayne's arithmetic.
2. **The turned portrait.** Before a major decision, a Long Gallery portrait
   is found facing the wall. Crew read omen; Cole reads morale sabotage;
   Rooke, tellingly, demands to know who touched the frame. A small mystery
   that introduces the flagship's culture — and the wrong suspect.
3. **One foothold only.** Any plan touching Hesperus Prime II, the belt, or
   the Prime IV ruin runs through Var's manifest: the colony supplies cannot
   be split. Var will say so, precisely and publicly, forcing the
   commitment question early.
4. **Fuel at the gas giant.** The flotilla's practical refueling point is
   Hesperus Prime V — where the quiet ship lurks (state/world_state.md;
   lore/factions/the_silent_tithe.md). Simple logistics steers the player
   toward first contact.
5. **A garbled sending.** Weeks in, Rhee delivers a mangled astropathic
   fragment from Calixis — legal phrases, a creditor's name, the word
   "coercion" — the first faint signal of the Displaced Line's paper siege
   (lore/factions/the_displaced_line.md), arriving too broken to act on.

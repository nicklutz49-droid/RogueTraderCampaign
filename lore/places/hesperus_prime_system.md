# THE HESPERUS PRIME SYSTEM

> **Canon status:** GM reference — not yet revealed in play. State files record what the player knows.

The first system inside the Vortigern Passage and the flotilla's arrival point
(campaign Day 1 — see `state/campaign_log.md`, Entry 001). Everything below is
what long-range auspex, augur sweeps, and vox-intercept can establish from the
translation point in the outer system, plus GM notes on what each body offers
the Vharadan expedition. The flotilla has surveyed nothing up close; no orders
have yet been given.

Sabra Vortigern named the star in 512.M39 "for its evening-gold stars" — see
`lore/places/hesperus_reach.md` for the region, its dead colonies, and the
becalmed warp.

## Hesperus Prime — the star

**Auspex:** A white-gold main-sequence star, stable and unremarkable, its light
faintly amber at range — the "evening-gold" of Vortigern's survey logs. Solar
weather is mild; augur interference minimal.

**For the flotilla:** A kindly star by void standards. Plasma-drive transits
between bodies are days to weeks, not months. Navigator Vor Kaas notes the
system sits in warp-shadow like everything in the Reach: shallow, quiet,
"becalmed seas" (`lore/places/hesperus_reach.md`).

## I — "Cinder"

**Auspex:** A molten rock ball skimming the star, its sunward face a slag sea.
No atmosphere worth the name. Uninhabitable by anything.

**For the flotilla:** Nothing. Charted and dismissed. Junior auspex-adepts have
already taken to calling a hopeless task "a Cinder posting."

## II — "Veyra" (the lost world) — HOOK 1

**Auspex:** Terran-standard garden world. Cloud bands, blue oceans, green
continents — and city-glow on the night side. Vox intercepts crackle with a
drifted Low Gothic dialect: troop movements, freight schedules, what sound like
war bulletins. Millions of souls, feuding nation-states, pre-warp industry.
Faint wireless traffic only; no orbital anything.

**For the flotilla:** The prize and the problem. A populated human world that
has never heard of the God-Emperor is subjects, labor, regiments, and tithe —
or a fifteen-millennia tinderbox. Sister Marguerite can crack the dialect;
nobody else aboard can. One starship descending from these skies will not be
ignored. Full canon (nations, the Grey War, the Church of the Return, deeper
secrets): `lore/places/hesperus_prime_ii_veyra.md`.

## III — "the Red Waste"

**Auspex:** Thin-atmosphere red rock; dust storms; and on a northern plateau, a
grid of geometric returns — an Imperial waystation of M39 Vortigern-era
pattern, long cold. No power signatures, no life, no vox.

**For the flotilla:** The safest first landing in the system. Minor salvage:
structural plasteel, possibly intact cogitator cores or void-tanks, and
whatever record of the Vortigern colonies the dead station kept. A cheap place
to blood the house troops, test the landers, and plant the first Vharadan flag
without consequence. Probably.

## The asteroid belt

**Auspex:** Dense and mineral-rich between III and IV — augur returns flag
heavy-metal concentrations well above Calixis-norm, plus water ice. No beacons,
no claims, no mining scars. Untouched since the stars cooled.

**For the flotilla:** The Profit hook. With the treasury Strained
(`state/dynasty_ledger.md`), ore and void-ice are the difference between an
expedition and an empire. Exploiting it needs infrastructure the flotilla does
not carry — or arrangements with someone who does. Note that any belt claim is
exactly the sort of thing House Baltrane's tonnage will eventually contest
(`lore/factions/imperial_actors.md`).

## IV — the grey world and the ruin — HOOK 2

**Auspex:** An airless grey world, cratered and dead. In orbit: a shattered
ring of debris that returns *geometric* — angles too regular for rock, too
wrong for Imperial work — plus one largely intact fragment, kilometers long.
The auspex logs cannot classify the material. Magos Errant Vex-Han-9 has
reviewed the returns and is, in the words of the watch officer, "highly
agitated"; he has requested priority survey rights in every meeting since
translation.

**For the flotilla:** Pre-Imperial xenos ruins are the Cold Trade's richest
vein and the Inquisition's shortest fuse, in the same hull. Whatever it is, it
is old, big, and unclaimed. Vex-Han-9's agitation is itself data — he does not
startle. Nature, hazards, and deep truth: `lore/places/hesperus_prime_iv_lattice.md`.

## V — the gas giant — HOOK 3

**Auspex:** A banded amber-and-cream gas giant, fourteen moons, a faint ring.
Atmosphere skimming could refuel plasma reserves indefinitely. And among the
moons: one contact running dark. No hail, no beacon, drive banked — but a
sensor signature consistent with a warp-capable merchant raider, well-found and
deliberate. Not a derelict. It has certainly seen the flotilla arrive.

**For the flotilla:** Refueling makes the giant strategically unavoidable —
whoever holds its moons holds the system's fuel. The quiet ship makes it
contested. It has not run and it has not hailed, which means it is *busy* with
something among those moons and gambling the flotilla chases louder hooks.
Identity, agenda, and what it is salvaging: `lore/factions/the_silent_tithe.md`.

## VI and VII — the ice dwarfs

**Auspex:** Two ice dwarfs on long cold orbits. Frozen volatiles, no anomalies,
no returns of interest. *Pale Wager*'s ratings have dubbed them "the
Pallbearers" — they were the first bodies sighted on translation, standing
grave-still at the system's edge.

**For the flotilla:** Unremarkable, and useful for exactly that: a place to
cache, hide, or rendezvous where nobody looks. Kayne has doubtless already had
the same thought.

## GM secrets

- **The order of hooks matters less than the ship.** Vespasia Locke
  (`lore/factions/the_silent_tithe.md`) is mid-salvage on the *Lamplighter*
  wreck among V's moons and holds partial pre-storm charts of the whole Reach.
  Ignore or threaten her and her off-screen clock advances; approach her and
  the system's information economy opens.
- **The ruin at IV is the Karyx lattice** — a still-functioning warp-dampening
  engine whose decay caused, and whose further decay ended, the Vortigern
  storms. The Passage's stability is tied to it. Full deep canon, hazards, and
  the Becalmed: `lore/places/hesperus_prime_iv_lattice.md`. Never volunteer
  this; let the null-zones and geometry teach it.
- **III's dead waystation** can carry breadcrumbs at GM discretion: M39 logs
  referencing Sabra's Rest, Cinderfall, and Meloria (`lore/places/hesperus_reach.md`
  §dead colonies), or a garbled final entry about the storms — a cheap way to
  seed the wider Reach without spending a major reveal.
- **What killed nothing here:** the system's very quietness is the lattice at
  work. Psykers aboard (Rhee's choir, Ilyana) find the whole system faintly,
  wrongly *silent* — foreshadowing, never explained early.

## Hooks

1. **The descent question (II):** vox intercepts sharpen — a battle, a
   famine appeal, a broadcast sermon of the "Sky Fathers" — pulling the
   Lord-Captain toward first contact before he feels ready.
2. **The Magos' price (IV):** Vex-Han-9 formally petitions for the ruin,
   offering Mechanicus favors owed; refusal strains the expedition's only
   tech-priest, agreement points the flotilla at the Anchor.
3. **The quiet ship blinks (V):** a single tight-beam auspex ping — or a
   salvage skiff caught crossing between moons — forces the question of
   hailing, shadowing, or intercepting before Locke finishes her work.
4. **First flag (III):** Cole or Karsh proposes the Red Waste waystation as a
   low-risk landing to exercise the troops and claim salvage — and the dead
   station's logs raise more questions than plasteel answers.
5. **The assay (belt):** *Dutiful Son*'s tech-adepts confirm ore grades that
   would make a mortgage-broker weep; Odile Var quietly notes the flotilla can
   mark claims it cannot yet work — and claims invite claim-jumpers.

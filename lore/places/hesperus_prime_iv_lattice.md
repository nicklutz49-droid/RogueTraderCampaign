# HESPERUS PRIME IV — THE LATTICE

> **Canon status:** GM reference — not yet revealed in play. State files record what the player knows.

This file **decides the ruin's nature**. The state file (`state/world_state.md`)
records only "geometric orbital debris, pre-Imperial or older" — that is all
the player knows as of campaign Day 1. Everything below is fixed GM canon.

## The world beneath

Hesperus Prime IV is an airless grey ball of regolith and old impact glass —
no atmosphere, no resources worth a lighter's fuel, nothing to explain why
anything was ever built here. That absence is the first clue: the Karyx did
not come for the world. They came for the *position*. IV's orbit sits at what
Vex-Han-9 will eventually (and unhappily) describe as a point of "aetheric
convenience" — a place where the skin between realspace and the warp could be
stitched.

## What the ruin is (DECIDED)

The ruin is the wreck of a vast orbital lattice built by an extinct xenos
species the record will come to call the **Karyx** — their own name for
themselves is lost; the label comes from glyph-clusters on Vex-Han-9's
Cold Trade data-lattice (see `lore/people/vex_han_9.md`) that render,
approximately, as *karyx*. The structure is pre-Imperial by tens of
millennia.

Its function: a **warp-dampening engine**. The Karyx built it to becalm the
warp across the entire Hesperus Reach — and it *still partially works*. This
is the true cause of the Reach's shallow, quiet warp, its muffled astropathy,
and its starvation of daemonic incursion (see `lore/places/hesperus_reach.md`).
The lattice is not a ruin that sits in the Reach. The Reach as everyone
experiences it **is the lattice, working**.

Why the Karyx built a cage is the deepest secret in the campaign — see
**GM secrets** below.

## What auspex shows now (Day 1 — safe to narrate)

From the flotilla's current position, standard augur returns give:

| Return | Reading |
|---|---|
| Debris ring | A shattered ring of geometric fragments in stable orbit of IV — arcs, spars, polyhedral shards, some kilometers across. Too regular for rock, too old for Imperial work. |
| Composition | Crystalline-metallic, no match in the *Intemperance*'s pattern-libraries. Cogitators return NULL REFERENT and, on one memorable pass, a prayer of apology. |
| The intact mass | One fragment reads largely whole — kilometers long. (This is **the Anchor**, though nobody has named it yet.) |
| Energy | Faint, rhythmic power fluctuation from the intact mass, period ~31 hours. Not dead. Not awake. |
| Anomalies | Auspex sweeps of the ring return *inconsistent geometry* — the same fragment measures differently on successive passes. Adepts blame instrument drift. It is not instrument drift. |

Vex-Han-9's agitation (noted in `state/npcs.md`) needs no secret knowledge to
justify on-screen: an unknown xenos megastructure is reason enough. His real
reason is the glyph match (below).

## What exploration reveals — in layers

Reveal in this order; each layer should cost something (time, casualties,
equipment, nerve).

1. **The ring (first sortie).** Dead fragments, drifting. Surfaces carved
   with dense geometric glyphs — the same script family as Vex-Han-9's
   data-lattice. First hard proof of a builder-species; first use of the
   word "Karyx." Salvage here is scientifically priceless, commercially
   nearly worthless: the material resists every tool, and severed fragments
   *stop doing anything*.
2. **The Anchor, outer shell.** The intact fragment: kilometers long,
   geometrically wrong to human eyes — angles that sum incorrectly, edges
   the gaze slides off. Interior atmosphere: none. Interior gravity: yes,
   sourceless, politely oriented to whoever enters. First contact with
   **guardian constructs** (dormant unless intruded upon) and the outer
   **null-zones**.
3. **The Anchor, deep structure.** Working machinery. Chambers that are
   larger inside than outside; corridors that reconnect behind the explorers.
   Glyph-walls that, properly translated (Vex-Han-9's lattice plus, perhaps,
   Sister Marguerite's linguistics — see `lore/people/sister_marguerite.md`),
   begin to describe *what the machine does*: a becalming. A cage.
4. **The heart (many sessions away).** The Anchor's core: the surviving
   regulator of the whole Reach-wide engine. Here the deep secrets (below)
   become legible — and touchable. Do not let the party reach this quickly.

## Hazards

| Hazard | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Guardian constructs** | Crystalline, geometric, faceted things — no faces, no limbs in any honest sense. Dormant for millennia; wake on intrusion, escalating with depth and with damage done to the structure. They do not pursue beyond the lattice. They do not negotiate. They are *maintenance*, and intruders are debris. |
| **Null-zones** | Volumes where warp-technology fails outright: vox falls silent, Navigator and psyker senses go blind (terrifying for Ilyana — see `lore/people/ilyana_vor_kaas.md`), Geller-adjacent systems misbehave, and Vex-Han-9's more esoteric instruments simply stop. Machine-spirits sulk for days afterward. |
| **Wrong geometry** | Structural traps of pure architecture: spaces larger inside than out, corridors that reconnect to themselves, stairs that descend twice and arrive above. Not malicious — the Karyx simply did not build for human minds. Mapping parties WILL get lost; unlogged tether-lines are the difference between a scare and a funeral. |
| **The 31-hour pulse** | The Anchor's power rhythm. During the pulse's crest, null-zones swell and constructs are more easily roused. Attentive explorers can learn to time their sorties. |

## Who knows what (Day 1)

- **Vex-Han-9** — glyph match only. His auctioned data-lattice bears the same
  script; he has told no one aboard. If he confirms a major find here, his
  private channel to Archmagos Ruuk starts a clock — see
  `lore/people/vex_han_9.md` and `lore/factions/imperial_actors.md`.
- **Lady-Captain Locke** — garbled fragments of Sabra Vortigern's pre-storm
  survey notes, salvaged from the *Lamplighter*, that *link the ruin to the
  Passage storms* — incomplete, tantalizing, wrong in places. See
  `lore/factions/the_silent_tithe.md`. She would trade on this knowledge
  before she would share it.
- **Nobody else knows anything.** Not the Veyrans, not the dead colonies,
  not the Administratum. The party can become the third holders of this
  secret in two millennia.

## GM secrets

**Reveal these slowly, over many sessions, in the layer order above.**

1. **The lattice failure caused the Vortigern storms.** ~Two millennia ago
   (743.M39) a section of the lattice decayed and failed catastrophically.
   The becalmed warp roared back at the region's boundary — and that roar
   *is* the Vortigern storms that sealed the Reach and swallowed Sabra
   Vortigern's last expedition.
2. **Its recent decay reopened the Passage.** Further decay (~816.M41)
   changed the shape of the boundary turbulence, and the Passage fell quiet.
   The reopening was not providence, not the Emperor's will, not luck. It
   was rot.
3. **The Passage's stability is tied to the lattice.** This is the campaign's
   great strategic lever. Repairing, damaging, salvaging, or "optimizing" the
   lattice could re-seal the Reach — stranding the dynasty and everyone
   else — or fling the Passage wide and stable, making Aurelian the
   gatekeeper of a sector. Nobody may pull this lever by accident; telegraph
   the stakes before any irreversible act.
4. **The Becalmed.** The Karyx built a cage because something in the Reach's
   warp needed caging: a dormant warp-presence the lore names only **the
   Becalmed**. Its nature is deliberately unresolved *even in GM canon* — do
   not privately decide it, either. Locked facts: it is vast; it sleeps
   because the lattice makes it sleep; and during the Passage transit it
   half-noticed Ilyana Vor Kaas, who has told no one (see
   `lore/people/ilyana_vor_kaas.md`).

**Pacing guidance — the Becalmed is the campaign's longest game.** Chaos in
this campaign whispers (per `CLAUDE.md`); the Becalmed barely even whispers.
Use it as: a texture in Ilyana's nightmares, a repeated glyph the translators
cannot place, a dream shared by two ratings who have never met, one sentence
in Vortigern's recovered notes. Never a voice, never a bargain, never a
stat-block. Every point of lattice decay lets it stir a fraction more —
which gives Aurelian, eventually, the worst temptation of all: the lattice
keeps the Reach *safe*, and the lattice keeps the Reach *closed*. NEVER rush
it on-screen. If in doubt, delay the reveal another arc.

## Hooks

1. **Vex-Han-9 petitions for an expedition** — on Day 1 he is already
   agitating. His price for continued cooperation may become access to the
   ruin; his glyph-lattice makes any sortie he joins vastly more productive
   (and starts his off-screen clock ticking toward Archmagos Ruuk).
2. **First blood on the Anchor** — a boarding sortie wakes a guardian
   construct; survivors return with casualties, a glyph-rubbing, and the
   first hint that the ruin is not dead. Establishes the site's rules the
   hard way.
3. **Locke's auction** — Vespasia Locke offers to sell "Vortigern's survey
   annex on the fourth planet" (garbled, partial, genuine). A cheap way to
   hand the party secret #1 in corrupted form — and to entangle them with
   the *Silent Tithe*.
4. **The null-zone incident** — a mapping team's vox goes dead inside a
   null-zone during a pulse-crest; a rescue must go in blind, without psyker
   senses or reliable auspex. Ilyana's blindness inside the zone shakes her
   badly — a doorway to her secret.
5. **The lever revealed** — once the party grasps secret #3, every other
   faction's interest in the ruin transforms: Ruuk wants to study it, Locke
   wants to broker it, Kest may call it a work of the Archenemy, and the
   Becalmed... waits. The ruin stops being a site and becomes the Reach's
   throne-room.

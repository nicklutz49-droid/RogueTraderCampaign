# THE HESPERUS REACH

> **Canon status:** GM reference — not yet revealed in play. State files record what the player knows.

A sector-scale region of the void beyond the **Vortigern Passage**, named by its
discoverer "for its evening-gold stars." Two millennia sealed behind warp storms;
reopened barely six months before campaign start. Dead Imperial colonies, lost
human worlds that have never heard the God-Emperor's name, untouched mineral
wealth, and unknown threats — all of it beyond the Administratum's grasp for
decades to come. **First Warrant-holders write the rules.**

Regional canon here. System-level detail: `lore/places/hesperus_prime_system.md`.

## History

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **512.M39** | Rogue Trader **Sabra Vortigern**, aboard her survey flagship the **Lamplighter**, charts the warp route later called the Vortigern Passage and surveys the region she names the Hesperus Reach. |
| **512–743.M39** | Two centuries of Imperial settlement in her wake: the shrine-fort colony of **Sabra's Rest**, the promethium-and-ore world **Cinderfall**, the agri-colony **Meloria**. |
| **743.M39** | The Passage slams shut — sudden, violent warp storms at the region's boundary. The colonies are cut off. Vortigern's last expedition, the *Lamplighter* with it, never returns. |
| **743.M39–816.M41** | Two millennia of silence. The Reach becomes a footnote in Calixian cartularies; the colonies are written off as tithe-losses and forgotten. |
| **Late 816.M41** | The Passage reopens as abruptly as it closed. The rush begins — freebooters first, the Imperium's slow machinery behind them (`lore/factions/imperial_actors.md`). |
| **817.M41** | The Vharadan flotilla translates into the Hesperus Prime system. Campaign Day 1. Nothing after this moment has happened. |

### Sabra Vortigern and the Lamplighter

Vortigern was a charter-and-chart Rogue Trader — no conqueror, a mapmaker with
a Warrant. Her genius was routes: she sold passage, not plunder, and the
Passage that bears her name made her house briefly rich. The *Lamplighter* was
her instrument — a survey vessel crusted with augur arrays, her prow-lantern
kept lit "so the dark would know we were coming back." When the storms took
her, Vortigern was aboard, running one last deep survey. Her house did not
survive the loss; her charts scattered or burned.

The *Lamplighter*'s wreck has been found — but not by the player. See
`lore/factions/the_silent_tithe.md` for who holds it and what was in it.

## The becalmed warp (LOCKED region rules)

The warp runs **shallow and quiet** across the entire Reach — eerily so.
Navigators call it "becalmed seas" and do not like it; a sea without weather
feels like a held breath. Consequences, all locked:

- **Warp travel within the Reach is slow but unusually safe.** Transits take
  longer than charts suggest, but Geller incidents, warp-spawned phenomena,
  and translation accidents are rare. Voyages feel like poling a barge across
  a millpond.
- **Astropathy is MUFFLED.** Sendings within the Reach are short-ranged and
  effortful; messages to or from Calixis take *weeks* and arrive garbled and
  partial. Astropath Transcendant Maddalen Rhee describes it as "singing into
  wet felt."
- **Daemonic incursion is rare and weak here.** Chaos in this campaign
  whispers; in the Reach it barely does that.

### Campaign consequences of the muffled-astropathy rule (GM: enforce these)

1. **Nobody phones home quickly — spies included.** Sister Marguerite's
   reports (`lore/people/sister_marguerite.md`), Cassia Renn's dossiers
   (`lore/factions/the_displaced_line.md`), Ilyana's house correspondence,
   and Vex-Han-9's encrypted cant to the Lathes all crawl at the same weeks-
   long, lossy pace. Every off-screen clock that depends on word reaching
   Calixis inherits this delay.
2. **No rescue, no reinforcement, no appeal.** Aurelian cannot summon help,
   and neither can his enemies. Whatever happens in the Reach is settled in
   the Reach.
3. **News arrives stale and wrong.** Garbled sendings are a GM tool: partial
   truths, misdated warnings, orders overtaken by events.
4. **Local vox and courier ships matter.** Within a system, vox rules; between
   systems, a fast ship beats an astropath as often as not. Controlling
   couriers and relay arrangements is real power here.
5. **The Passage mouth is the chokepoint.** The way-station rising on the
   Calixis side (**Anchorage Vigil**, `lore/factions/imperial_actors.md`)
   will eventually broker all reliable communication in and out.

The Navigators' unease is not superstition. The becalming is not natural —
but WHY is answered nowhere in this file. Hints only; the deep cause lives
in `lore/places/hesperus_prime_iv_lattice.md` (see GM secrets).

## The dead colonies of the Vortigern era

Three colonies took root before the storms. All three appear on the pre-storm
charts salvaged from the *Lamplighter* (`lore/factions/the_silent_tithe.md`) —
along with routes to them nobody else possesses. Surface impressions below;
true fates under GM secrets.

| Colony | Founded as | Apparent state (from orbit / rumor) |
|---|---|---|
| **Sabra's Rest** | Vortigern's first landing; shrine-fort colony | Signs of habitation: hearth-smoke, terraced fields, fortified shrine-keeps |
| **Cinderfall** | Promethium and ore extraction world | Silent. Orbital docks dark but intact. No life-signs from a distance |
| **Meloria** | Agri-colony; a gentle world | Inhabited and cultivated — vast grain lands, no vox traffic in Gothic anyone recognizes at first pass |

## The politics of an empty frontier

The Administratum cannot project power into the Reach for decades. There are
no tithe assessments, no sector courts, no Battlefleet patrols — only whatever
order a Warrant of Trade imposes. The Vharadan Warrant grants rights of trade,
conquest, and colonization "beyond the Emperor's light"; the Reach is the
purest expression of that phrase in the Calixian records.

- **Claims are made by deed, not filing.** A colony planted, a route charted,
  a world brought to compliance — these will be the facts on the ground when
  the Imperium's surveyors finally arrive (Year 1–2 of the traffic clock,
  `lore/factions/imperial_actors.md`).
- **Rivals operate under the same freedom.** Lady-Captain Vespasia Locke
  holds a genuine minor Warrant; House Baltrane is coming with a bigger one.
  Between Warrant-holders, precedent is set by whoever acts first and holds
  what they take.
- **The muffled warp is the guarantor.** No one can appeal over anyone's
  head. Every dispute in the Reach is local, personal, and final.

## GM secrets

- **Why the warp is becalmed:** the full answer is the orbital ruin at
  Hesperus Prime IV — see `lore/places/hesperus_prime_iv_lattice.md`. In
  THIS file, only hints: the becalming is uniform, artificial-feeling, and
  centered somewhere; the 743.M39 storms erupted at the region's *boundary*,
  not its heart; the Passage's closing and reopening are two symptoms of one
  cause. Navigators who linger in the Reach develop a quiet dread they
  cannot articulate. Ilyana Vor Kaas already has a reason of her own
  (`lore/people/ilyana_vor_kaas.md`).
- **Sabra's Rest (fate LOCKED):** survivors persist — regressed into feudal
  shrine-keeper clans, population ~200,000, who await "the Lamplighter's
  return." Two millennia of oral tradition have fused Vortigern, her ship,
  and the God-Emperor's light into one promise: a lantern will descend from
  heaven and the long night will end. A ship arriving with the right words —
  or the right lie — inherits a faith ready-made.
- **Cinderfall (fate LOCKED):** dead — but its orbital docks and refinery
  infrastructure are intact and salvageable, a ready-built industrial base
  worth more than most prizes in the Reach. What killed it is NOT obvious
  from orbit, and this canon deliberately does not say. GM: leave it open;
  something did, and the docks' pristine condition should feel wrong. Play
  it as horror-mystery when explored, and decide the truth only when the
  campaign needs it.
- **Meloria (fate LOCKED):** alive, insular, ~3 million souls. Two millennia
  of isolation bred a rigid grain-tithe theocracy; they remember the
  Imperium only as legend — the "Granary Lords beyond the sky" to whom
  tithes were once owed and will one day be owed again. A returning starship
  is either the legend fulfilled or an impostor to be resisted; their
  hierarchs will split over which.
- **The pre-storm charts:** Locke's salvage from the *Lamplighter* lists all
  three colonies plus routes nobody else has, and garbled survey notes
  linking the Hesperus Prime IV ruin to the storms. Whoever holds those
  charts skips years of blind surveying (`lore/factions/the_silent_tithe.md`).

## Hooks

1. **The chart monopoly.** The fastest way to the dead colonies runs through
   Vespasia Locke's salvaged charts — buy them, trade for them, or take
   them. Each approach sets the tone of the Vharadan–Locke relationship.
2. **A lantern from heaven.** First contact with Sabra's Rest hands Aurelian
   200,000 believers who have waited two thousand years for a ship. Claim
   the prophecy, correct it, or exploit it — and note that Arch-Confessor
   Kest (`lore/factions/imperial_actors.md`) will eventually have opinions.
3. **The Cinderfall prize.** Intact orbital docks and refineries would jump
   the dynasty's Profit Factor and industrial base in one stroke — if
   whatever emptied the world is truly gone. A perfect bold-option/wise-
   option dilemma for the Hunger.
4. **The grain problem.** Feeding colonies, crews, and any future subject
   worlds makes Meloria's harvests strategic. Negotiating with a theocracy
   that may worship or despise him tests Usurper's Instinct rather than guns.
5. **Weeks of silence.** Use the muffled astropathy actively: a garbled
   warning from Calixis arrives half-legible; a spy's report misses its
   window; a rival acts on stale news. The Reach's isolation should be felt
   at the table, not just noted.

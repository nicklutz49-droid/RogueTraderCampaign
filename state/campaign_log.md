## Entry 001 — Day 1 of the Hesperus Expedition (campaign opening)

**Situation:** Lord-Captain Aurelian Vharadan, having seized the family
Warrant and liquidated the dynasty's remaining assets, has brought his
flotilla (*Intemperance*, *Pale Wager*, *Dutiful Son*) through the newly
reopened Vortigern Passage into the Hesperus Reach. The transit was rough —
two weeks of Geller field alarms — but Navigator Ilyana Vor Kaas brought
all three ships through intact.

**On arrival at Hesperus Prime, auspex revealed three hooks:**
1. Hesperus Prime II: inhabited pre-warp human lost colony, feuding
   nation-states, millions of souls
2. Hesperus Prime IV: ancient orbital ruin (Vex-Han-9 highly agitated)
3. A quiet ship near the gas giant — possible rival Rogue Trader, no hail

**Decisions made:** NONE YET — the Lord-Captain's first orders in the
Reach are pending. Resume here.

**Pending consequences:** All three hooks live. The quiet ship knows the
flotilla has arrived.

## Entry 002 — Day 5 of the Hesperus Expedition

**Decision:** The Lord-Captain's first order in the Reach: no exploring with
a potential danger at our backs. The full flotilla burned for the gas giant
(Hesperus Prime V) to confront the quiet ship, then refuel.

**Events:**
- Four-day transit. The quiet ship held position and let the fleet come.
- Day 3: auspex resolved her anchorage — she is salvaging a large, ancient
  Imperial wreck (pattern too old for our registries) in a debris field
  trailing the outer moons. Salvage gangs active; she has been at it months.
- Day 4: she hailed FIRST, at long range, before any weapons-rite — voice
  only. Identified herself: **Lady-Captain Vespasia Locke**, vessel
  ***Silent Tithe***, Warrant of House Locke of Malfi. Demonstrated
  unsettling knowledge: quoted the *Intemperance*'s gun count, muster, even
  bow trim. Claims a "lawful prior salvage claim" on the wreck (calls the
  site "the Bonefield"). Offered refueling guidance (third moon) as a
  "courtesy owed between Warrants" and asked our tenders keep clear of her
  wreck.

**Decisions pending:** How to answer Locke — parley in person, assert
Warrant precedence over her claim, trade courtesy for knowledge, refuel
while quietly testing her escape line, or other. RESUME HERE.

**Pending consequences:** Locke has read our strength to the rivet; we know
almost nothing of hers. The wreck's identity is unknown to us. Veyra and
the Prime IV ruin wait untouched; Vex-Han-9's patience is spending down.

## Entry 003 — Days 5–14 of the Hesperus Expedition

**Decisions:** Accepted Locke's refueling courtesy while keeping full auspex
watch on her; did not contest her salvage claim; whole fleet then departed
for Veyra (Hesperus Prime II).

**Events:**
- Days 5–8: refueled at the gas giant's third moon. Auspex profile of the
  *Silent Tithe*: overtuned drives (faster than anything we fly), gun decks
  rebuilt into sensor/interceptor farms — "an ear with very good boots"
  (Cole). Her wreck is a vast pre-storm Imperial SURVEY vessel; site ringed
  with lawful Locke claim-buoys. Kayne asked twice to drift past it.
- Day 8: Locke sent a free "sample of the house's wares": Veyra has three
  powers (Concordat of Bray — industrial, winning; Kingdom of Tessene —
  martial, losing; Shorelord Compact — neutral profiteers), an 11-year war
  ("the Grey War"), and a planetary religion that says the sky-people WILL
  RETURN. Her warning: "You won't be arriving. You'll be returning." Trade
  channel offered. We lost eyes on her at departure.
- Days 9–14: transit. Vex-Han-9 now at nine formal petitions re: the ruin.
  Marguerite cracked Veyric (drifted Low Gothic) from intercepts — she can
  interpret, soon pass. She is now the expedition's indispensable translator.
- Day 14: high orbit over Veyra. Confirmed from orbit/intercepts: early
  industrial tech (rail, artillery, dirigibles, gas shells, crackle-vox),
  Grey Marches front visibly active, Church of the Return ubiquitous in
  broadcasts on all sides.

**Decisions pending:** First contact — Tessene (come as prophecy), Bray
(come as contractor), the Compact (come as merchant), hold and listen, or
other. RESUME HERE.

**Pending consequences:** Locke gleans on unwatched, forming her read of us.
Every contact choice on Veyra tilts the Grey War. Vex-Han-9's patience is a
meter running down.
